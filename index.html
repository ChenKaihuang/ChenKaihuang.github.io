Kaihuang Chen

Hong Kong SAR, China

I work on large-scale optimization and GPU-accelerated scientific computing. My research focuses on operator splitting methods and algorithm design for linear and quadratic programming. I completed my Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University in February 2026.

Research interests

  • Large-scale convex optimization
  • Linear and quadratic programming
  • Operator splitting methods
  • GPU-accelerated optimization
  • Sparse scientific computing
  • High-performance computing
